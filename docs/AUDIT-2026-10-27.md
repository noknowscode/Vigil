## Correctness

1. **Dark theme (measured, WCAG ratios; corrected 2026-10-28).** `PetBackdrop` always clears to light `#DFEAF0`
   (`PetBackdrop.cs:73`), so the fixed dark hero inks stay readable. The failures are theme-aware text over that
   always-light panel: agenda and reminder initials use `PrimaryBrush` (dark `#DCEB9D`) at **1.05:1**
   (`BoardPage.xaml:42, 59`). The reminder card in dark theme fails AA for its 10-11 px text: body 3.56:1,
   overdue label 3.36:1 (`CloverLime` dark `#384C2C`). Photo panels stay light in dark theme by design or by accident.
2. **French regressions.** `Monitor_LastRecorded` was `Loc.F`, now hardcoded English (`RecordModel.cs`, `LastReading`).
   Also English-only: `WeightDate`, `MedicationSummary/Detail`, `PaneTitle` (`RecordModel.cs:193`),
   `DaySummary` (`BoardModel.cs`), all of `ParentSnapshot` (`ParentPreviewModel.cs:16-26`).
3. **Dead code.** Nothing navigates to the `Dose` route (`App.xaml.cs:204`): `DoseModel`/`DosePage` are unreachable,
   and `RecordModel.cs:355-388` copies their logic. `Styles/ColorPaletteOverride.xaml` is no longer referenced.
4. **No-op styling.** `CharacterSpacing` setters (`Themes/Clover.xaml:35-62`, `BoardPage.xaml:147`) do nothing on Skia.
5. **Font.** `CloverEditorialFont` = `Georgia`, Windows-only; other heads fall back.
